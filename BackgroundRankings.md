# Portfolio Background Design & Rankings
Here is the ranking of mathematical, pretty, and serious background ideas for the portfolio website:
1. **The Space-Time Coordinate Grid (Warped Mesh)**
   - A Cartesian grid system that warps dynamically near the cursor.
   - Emulates gravity wells, general relativity, or mesh deformation.
   - Highly structured, professional, and interactive.
2. **Perlin/Simplex Flow Fields (Vector Fields)**
   - Tiny particles flowing along vectors generated by noise/trigonometric fields.
   - Fluid, organic, and mesmerizing. Excellent for showing dynamic movement.
3. **Supratik Bhowal's Dots**
   - A grid of dots attracted to the mouse cursor with spring-physics.
   - Bouncy, responsive, tactile, and minimal.
4. **Interactive Delaunay Triangulation / Voronoi Diagrams**
   - Dynamic cellular partitioning or network mesh connecting drifting points.
5. **Strange Attractors (Chaos Theory / Lorenz Attractor)**
   - Elegant, chaotic orbital lines traced in real-time.
6. **Fourier / Lissajous Wave Harmonics**
   - Overlapping trigonometric wave lines creating interference patterns.
